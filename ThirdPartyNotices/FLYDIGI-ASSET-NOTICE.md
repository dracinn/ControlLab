# Flydigi artwork notice

`Vader5Pro-Official.png` is the official Vader 5 Pro product render extracted
unchanged from Flydigi Space Station 4.1.2.17 for controller identification and
live input visualization.

`FlydigiDock2Pro-Official.jpg` is an official Charging Dock 2 Pro product render
published by Flydigi and used as the background for the dock lighting preview.

`FlydigiDock2Pro-Front.png` is a near-front product image distributed by
GadgetHyper and used to align the dock's triangular pixel preview.

`FlydigiDockDefaults/default_mapping_*.dat` are the factory LED mappings
distributed with Flydigi Space Station 4.2.0.9. ControlLab decodes only the
current factory animation for accurate Default-mode preview and restoration.
